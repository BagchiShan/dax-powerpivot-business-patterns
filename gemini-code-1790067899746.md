## 1. Store Size Segmentation Pattern

### Business Problem
Leaving square footage as a continuous variable clutters downstream slicers and Pivot Table reporting. Standardizing physical retail units into categorized tiers (Small, Medium, Large, Huge) enables clearer cohort comparisons and executive summaries.

### DAX Implementation
\`\`\`dax
supermarket_size = 
SWITCH(
    TRUE(),
    ISBLANK([total_sqft]), "Unknown",
    [total_sqft] > 35000, "Huge",
    [total_sqft] > 30000, "Large",
    [total_sqft] > 25000, "Medium",
    "Small"
)
\`\`\`

### Optimization Decisions
- **Sequential Logic:** Relies on top-down evaluation in `SWITCH(TRUE())` to remove redundant compound logical operators (`&&`).
- **Edge-Case Safeguards:** Placed `ISBLANK()` at the top to prevent missing records from being misclassified into numerical comparisons.
- **Maintainability:** Avoids deeply nested `IF()` statements, making the logic easier to audit and update.
